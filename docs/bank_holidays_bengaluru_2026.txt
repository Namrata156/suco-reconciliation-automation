WORKING-DAY RULE (owner, 2026-10-10)
------------------------------------
A day is NOT a working day if it is:
  - a Sunday,
  - the 2nd or 4th Saturday of the month (RBI: "All scheduled and
    non-scheduled banks will observe public holiday on second and fourth
    Saturdays"), or
  - a holiday in the list below.

"T+n" = the n-th working day after the transaction / business date T. If
T falls just before a non-working day, the deadline moves to the next
actual working day.
  Example: T = Fri 9 Oct 2026. Sat 10 Oct is a non-working day (2nd
  Saturday, and also Mahalaya Amavasye) and Sun 11 Oct is Sunday, so
  T+1 = Mon 12 Oct 2026.
Calendar-day TATs (7 / 35 days) are NOT shifted. That is the reading in
rule table v3.1, and it is still to be confirmed by the owner.

NOTES
-----
  - 2027: RBI has not published it yet (its year list stops at 2026).
    Deadlines that cross into 2027 can only skip Sundays and 2nd/4th
    Saturdays until it is added. The system should warn about this rather
    than silently assume.
  - Earlier years: deadlines for older data (e.g. 2025 transactions in
    late adjustments) need the 2025 list too. It is available from the
    same RBI page with Year = 2025.
  - Third-party sites gave conflicting dates (e.g. Ugadi 19 vs 20 Mar,
    Mahavir Jayanti 30 vs 31 Mar). RBI's own page is the source used here.
